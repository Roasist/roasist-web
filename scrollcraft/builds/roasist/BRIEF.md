## 1. The Eight Interview Topics

1. **Vibe (3-5 words) & Medium References:**
   - **Vibe:** Sharp, architectural, high-contrast, undeniable, kinetic.
   - **References:**
     1. *Rams (2018 documentary by Gary Hustwit)*: Industrial clarity, warm bone aluminum against matte carbon, zero decorative noise.
     2. *Bloomberg Businessweek Print Spreads*: Bold typographic contrast, asymmetric tension, hard factual comparisons.
     3. *Phase / Split-Stage Physical Exhibition*: Two simultaneous realities separated by a physical razor seam that shifts as the argument advances.

2. **The Scroll Journey (Section by Section):**
   - **Act 1 (Recognition / The Split Hero):** The viewport opens divided 50/50 by a vertical razor seam (`The Divider Chrome`). Left side (Dark Carbon `#0A0B0E`): *"Tahminle Harcanan Bütçe" (Clicks without attribution, vanity impressions, fragmented reports)*. Right side (Warm Bone `#F2EEE3` with multi-plane layered architectural depth): *"Mühendislikle Ölçülen Ciro" (Roasist 360° Growth Architecture)*. A giant display headline crosses both worlds.
   - **Act 2 (Tension / The Cost of Guesswork vs. Precision):** As the visitor scrolls, the divider shifts from 50% to 38%. Both columns advance through 3 pinned comparison states:
     - *State A:* Bulanık Veri (Kayıp Pikseller) vs. Server-Side GTM & GA4 Tam İzleme.
     - *State B:* Tek Kanallı Körlük vs. Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Yandex & SEO/AEO Sinerjisi.
     - *State C:* Aylık PDF Raporlar vs. Gerçek Zamanlı ROAS & Karlılık Mühendisliği.
   - **Act 3 (Turn / The 11-Service Ledger & URL Architecture):** Flow act with asymmetric `reveal` wipes on each side: Left shows why fragmented single-channel fixes fail; Right unveils Roasist's 11 core disciplines (360° Yönetim, Performans Pazarlaması, Google, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Yandex, Kurumsal SEO/AEO, Web Tasarım, Grafik Tasarım) as an interactive architectural ledger.
   - **Act 4 (Silence Before Peak):** A 0.85vh authored quiet breath where the noise on the left side stills to a single stark question (*"Reklam bütçenizin hangi yarısı boşa gidiyor?"*) while the seam vibrates with electric cobalt tension before the collapse.
   - **Act 5 (The Engineered Peak / The Seam Collapse & Interactive Proof):** Pinned peak (`3.4vh`, largest span on the page). The visitor's scroll drives the dividing seam all the way from 38% to `8% -> 0%`, physically wiping the dark "Guesswork" world off the screen as the bone-and-cobalt Roasist architecture claims the full stage, revealing the 29 verified client brands and interactive channel allocation matrix. Pointer/touch movement temporarily pulls the seam back to inspect the "Before/After" delta on demand.
   - **Act 6 (Commitment / The Resolved Winning Stage):** The divider has completely collapsed (`0%`). The winning warm-bone & cobalt stage holds the full viewport width. No spotlight, no magnetic toy button: a clean architectural inquiry ledger (*"Ücretsiz Büyüme Analizi Başlatın"*) and verified corporate credentials (`www.roasist.com`, İstanbul, Google & Meta Partner) sit permanently resolved with a hold cue.

3. **The Energy Curve:**

4. **The Feeling Curve & The Peak:**
   - **Act 1 (Recognition):** *Confrontation* — seeing the exact difference between chaotic ad spend (left) and engineered growth (right) simultaneously on the first screen, with layered architectural planes shifting at independent parallax rates.
   - **Act 2 (Tension):** *Unease turning to clarity* — the pinned stage holds while overlapping cues expose the hidden cost of broken attribution on the left and the exact technical remedy on the right as the divider begins tipping.
   - **Act 3 (Turn):** *Conviction* — hard `reveal` wipes replace agency jargon with Roasist's 11 concrete disciplines and verified 29-brand portfolio.
   - **Act 4 (Authored Silence):** *Anticipation* — a quiet 0.85vh pause with minimal copy before the seam gives way.
   - **Act 5 (The Peak):** *Triumph / Release* — the razor seam sweeps across the entire viewport and eliminates the dark side completely, expanding the layered Roasist stage to 100% width while real brand proofs lock into place.
   - **Act 6 (Commitment):** *Resolve* — the winning column owns the entire screen, holding steady on a concrete audit request form without fading out.
